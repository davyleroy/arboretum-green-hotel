# Staff guide: editing the Arboretum Green Hotel website

**Who this is for.** Hotel staff invited as Editor on Sanity Studio.
**What it covers.** How to change prices, hours, the homepage opening, photos, and what the yellow "to confirm" marker means.
**How long changes take.** About 2 minutes to go live after you click Publish.

The full French translation starts at section 11 below.

---

## 1. Logging in

1. Open `https://arboretumgreenhotel.rw/studio` in Chrome, Firefox or Edge. On a phone this works too, but editing is easier on a laptop.
2. Click **Google**, **GitHub** or **E-mail / Password** depending on which you signed up with.
3. The Studio opens with four or five groups on the left: **Hotel settings**, **Rooms**, **Dining**, **Meet**, **Discover**, **Photos**.

If a page says "You do not have access to this project", write to Davy (web@arboretumgreenhotel.rw) with a screenshot.

## 2. The golden rule

Every change you publish goes live on the public website about two minutes later. There is no "preview and then approve". So:

- **Save your typing as a draft** (the top of the document shows **Draft** while you type).
- Re-read it after a short break.
- Only click **Publish** when you are ready for guests to see it.

If you clicked Publish by mistake, you can edit the field again and Publish again. The site catches up in two minutes.

## 3. Change a room rate

1. Click **Rooms** on the left.
2. Click the room whose price changed.
3. Scroll to **Rate (RWF)**. Type the new number without spaces or commas. For example: `85000` not `85,000 RWF`.
4. Also tick off **To confirm** at the top if the price was previously marked as unconfirmed. See section 8 for what this marker means.
5. Click **Publish** (top of the page).

The new price appears on `/stay` in two minutes. Rates always display as `RWF 85,000` with the thousands separator added by the website for you.

## 4. Change dining opening hours

1. Click **Dining** on the left.
2. Click the outlet (Restaurant, Bar, or Coffee) you need.
3. Type the new hours into the **Hours** field. Any format staff normally use is fine: `07:00 to 22:00`, `7 am to 10 pm`, `Mon to Fri 10:00, weekends 09:00 to 23:00`.
4. If the hours were temporary or unconfirmed, tick **To confirm** off when they become final.
5. **Publish**.

## 5. Switch the homepage opening between the video and a photo

The homepage shows either a silent 15-second video of the gardens or a still photo. To change between them:

1. Click **Hotel settings** on the left.
2. Stay on the **Homepage opening** tab (the one open by default).
3. Change the field **Homepage opening shows** to either:
   - **A silent video loop**
   - **A photo**
4. **Publish**.

That is enough. The video is already loaded in the website. If you want a different photo, see section 7.

## 6. Replace a photo and write its alt text

Photos have "slots" (positions on the website). You replace the image in a slot; the slot stays the same.

1. Click **Photos** on the left.
2. Find the slot you want to change. Each slot has a name like `room-deluxe-1` or `home-hero-night`.
3. Click the slot, then click the photo. **Upload** a new image.
4. **Alt text** is a short sentence that describes the photo for visitors who cannot see it (blind guests, search engines, slow connections). Write one sentence per language:
   - **English** `A sunlit deluxe room with a four-poster bed and garden view.`
   - **Kinyarwanda** `Icyumba cya deluxe gifite igitanda n'inzu y'ubusitani.`
   - **French** `Une chambre deluxe ensoleillée avec lit à baldaquin et vue sur le jardin.`
5. **Publish**.

**Photo rules.**

- Only photos from the HAGY studio shoot or photos approved by Davy can go on the site.
- No people are visible in any photo (guests, staff or visitors) unless Davy has confirmed in writing.
- The photo must look as good on a phone as on a laptop: avoid landscape-heavy framing with the subject tiny in the middle.

If in doubt, send the photo to Davy before you upload it.

## 7. Change the hotel phone, email or address

1. **Hotel settings** on the left > **Contact** tab (top of the page).
2. Edit any of: **tagline**, **address**, **phones**, **WhatsApp**, **email**, **reception** text, **reply promise**.
3. **Publish**.

Note on phones: enter one per line in international format (`+250 781 600 065`). WhatsApp is just the digits with the country code, no `+` or spaces, for example `250781600065`.

## 8. What the yellow "to confirm" marker means

Some fields on the public site show in a lighter colour with a small marker next to them. That marker means the hotel has not confirmed this value yet, so the public website is showing it as "subject to confirmation" until you clear the marker.

To clear it: edit the field with the correct value, un-tick **To confirm** at the top of the document, then **Publish**.

The marker is there on purpose so staff never see outdated information presented as if it was final.

## 9. What publishing actually does

When you click **Publish** in the Studio:

1. Sanity (where the content lives) sends a signal to Vercel (where the website is hosted).
2. Vercel rebuilds the website with your change.
3. In about two minutes, every visitor sees the new version.

You do not need to refresh anything or log into another system. Just Publish and wait two minutes.

## 10. Who to call

- **Davy (Tenex)** for anything that breaks, anything you cannot find, or a question that is not in this guide. Email `web@arboretumgreenhotel.rw` or WhatsApp.
- **The hotel manager** for decisions about what to publish (prices, hours, offers).

Never share the Studio login with anyone who is not listed as a Member on sanity.io/manage. If a device is lost or a staff person leaves, tell Davy that day.

## 11. Version française (French version)

### Se connecter

1. Ouvrez `https://arboretumgreenhotel.rw/studio` dans Chrome, Firefox ou Edge. L'édition est plus facile sur ordinateur portable que sur téléphone.
2. Cliquez sur **Google**, **GitHub** ou **E-mail / Mot de passe** selon la méthode d'inscription.

### La règle d'or

Chaque modification publiée apparaît sur le site public environ deux minutes plus tard. Il n'y a pas d'étape "prévisualisation puis approbation". Alors :

- Enregistrez votre texte en brouillon (le document affiche **Draft / Brouillon** pendant que vous tapez).
- Relisez après une courte pause.
- Ne cliquez sur **Publier** que lorsque vous êtes prêt.

### Changer le tarif d'une chambre

1. Cliquez sur **Rooms** à gauche.
2. Sélectionnez la chambre concernée.
3. Dans **Rate (RWF)**, tapez le nouveau tarif sans espaces ni virgules. Exemple : `85000`.
4. Si le tarif était marqué "à confirmer", décochez **To confirm** en haut de la page.
5. Cliquez sur **Publish**.

### Changer les horaires d'un restaurant, bar ou café

1. Cliquez sur **Dining** à gauche.
2. Choisissez le lieu (Restaurant, Bar, Café).
3. Tapez les nouveaux horaires dans **Hours**. N'importe quel format compréhensible pour les clients fonctionne.
4. **Publish**.

### Alterner entre la vidéo et une photo sur la page d'accueil

1. **Hotel settings** à gauche > onglet **Homepage opening**.
2. Choisissez **A silent video loop** ou **A photo**.
3. **Publish**.

### Changer une photo et son texte alternatif

1. Cliquez sur **Photos** à gauche.
2. Trouvez la position à modifier (nom type `room-deluxe-1`).
3. Cliquez, puis téléversez la nouvelle image.
4. Le **texte alternatif** décrit la photo pour les visiteurs qui ne peuvent pas la voir. Une phrase par langue (anglais, kinyarwanda, français).
5. **Publish**.

**Règles photo.**
- Seules les photos du shoot HAGY ou approuvées par Davy sont autorisées.
- Aucun visage visible sauf confirmation écrite de Davy.

### Changer téléphone, e-mail ou adresse

**Hotel settings** > onglet **Contact**. Modifiez puis **Publish**. Téléphones un par ligne au format international. WhatsApp uniquement en chiffres avec l'indicatif, sans `+` ni espaces.

### Le marqueur jaune "to confirm"

Il signale qu'une valeur n'est pas encore confirmée par la direction. Pour l'enlever : modifier le champ avec la bonne valeur, décocher **To confirm** en haut, puis **Publish**.

### Ce que fait réellement "Publish"

Sanity envoie un signal à Vercel, qui reconstruit le site. Deux minutes plus tard, chaque visiteur voit la nouvelle version. Pas besoin de rafraîchir autre chose.

### Qui contacter

- **Davy (Tenex)** pour tout ce qui ne fonctionne pas ou n'est pas expliqué ici : `web@arboretumgreenhotel.rw` ou WhatsApp.
- **La direction** pour les décisions éditoriales (tarifs, horaires, promotions).

Ne partagez jamais votre identifiant avec une personne non listée comme Membre sur sanity.io/manage. Si un appareil est perdu ou un collaborateur quitte l'équipe, prévenez Davy le jour même.
